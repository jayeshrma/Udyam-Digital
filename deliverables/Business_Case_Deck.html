⚡ 4 Engines That Make KYC Near-Instant

  • 1. Conditional "Udyam Lite" Track: When bundle requires_gstin=N, waives GST Certificate—verifying via Udyam lookup + Signatory ID + Utility Bill (unlocks 47.2% of Micro leads).
  • 2. O/0 OCR Normalizer + Modulo-36 Checksum: Normalizes font confusion (0↔O in PAN letter positions 3–7, 12) before running Mod-36 check—auto-healing K02 & K08 while catching tampered K06.
  • 3. Live GSTN/Udyam Registry + DigiLocker: Instant verification against 3,534 GSTIN & 2,723 Udyam records (blocks Cancelled K01 & fixes missing/blurred files K04, K05).
  • 4. Remote Owner WhatsApp Aadhaar e-Sign: When an Office admin or Manager uploads docs (K08, K09 or the 53 unavailable-signatory drops), sends a 30-sec e-Sign magic link to the registered Proprietor's phone.

🔒 DPDP Act, 2023 Compliance by Design

  • Granular Vernacular Consent (Sec. 6): Itemised consent screen in 10 languages before document upload, with revocable audit log.
  • Strict Purpose Limitation (Sec. 4): KYC data used exclusively for B2B onboarding verification; zero public LLM training on PII.
  • Data Minimisation (Sec. 6(1)): Collects only bundle-mandated documents; pulls structured API tokens over paper where possible.
  • PII Masking & 72-Hr Auto-Purge: Masks GSTIN (27XUI******D1ZP) & Udyam in UI/logs; AES-256 at rest; purges raw PNG scans 72 hours after verification.
